# merely

**Software that does what it says. Merely made!**

Merely builds tools for people who are their own infrastructure.
This company emerged from my decade-long obsession with creating a 
different sort of browser. 

Different how? Well fellas, I'm gonna step out of professionalism to be 
honest and say I'm pissed. I, like the rest of us born in the 90s, 
was promised Star Trek CGI and got Google Chrome. No offense, 
as that's a tremendous product, but then we got every other browser,
and they were also Google Chrome, if not in engine than in spirit.

**Please, I beg, software that does not upsell**

It shouldn't cost you anything to make a note on your phone and access
it on your desktop. A business should not be built on an artificial cap 
on networked storage when all the devices networked by the product have 
plenty of their own storage! By and large we shit up the software to make 
money, and I'm damn pissed.

So I'm making software to delicately extract the shittiest parts of my
digital experience and replace them with MPL-2.0 implementations. I am
making extremely biased choices about what I wanna make. I may charge a dollar 
in app stores so I can live. But you can get the releases here for free anyway.

A few key notions emerged from my research:

- Tabs and pages are not sufficient GUI to represent browsing the web
- A browser should empower you to understand, access, and serve your content
- Communities should own and manage their data and computational resources.
- Coordination, curation, and learning are aligned with the development
of public, peer-governed inference. Those are also prime internet behaviors.
- The GUI that can do what tabs and pages can't is graphical data projection.
- Designing to a standard makes long-term solo development tractable. 
It's nice to have a guide. Thank you W3C!
- A web rendering engine is powerful and flexible enough to be its own GUI.
- A unified GPU device up and down the stack, an unhealthy attachment to wgpu,
and cross-platform ambitions work well together.

Some samples:

- **[Mere](https://github.com/merely-made/mere)** is a graph-first library for
  addressable content, spatial interfaces, local memory, and trusted exchange.
- **[Genet](https://github.com/merely-made/genet)** is an embeddable Rust web
  engine with static, interactive, scripted, and full-web profiles.
- **[Retinue](https://github.com/merely-made/retinue)** is a peer-to-peer and
  radio stack for identity, links, resources, and reliable streams.

- **[Turnstone](https://github.com/merely-made/turnstone)** is a graph-shaped
  browser for relating, curating, and cooperatively browsing the web.
- **[Woodshed](https://github.com/merely-made/woodshed)** is a practice toolkit
  for musicians.
- **[Cleromancy](https://github.com/merely-made/cleromancy)** is a local-first
  divination journal with replayable receipts.

- **[Isometry](https://github.com/merely-made/isometry)** is a pixel-art
  isometric virtual tabletop with peer-to-peer play.
